# PIP ART BRIEF

Hand this document to the 3D artist or follow it yourself in Spline or Pixcap.

## Character
Pip, a round baby parrot in the modeling clay style. Think Pixar short, matte Play-Doh. Not glossy plastic.

## Palette (exact hex values)
- Body and head: #B8E0D2 (mint)
- Belly patch: #FFE8D6 (peach)
- Wings: #A8C5E8 (sky blue)
- Beak and feet: #F2A488 (coral)
- Crest: three small feathers. Left #CBBBEA (lavender), middle #F4A6A6 (pink), right #A8C5E8 (sky)
- Eyes: #3D3D5C with one white highlight dot
- Cheeks: #F4A6A6 at about 40 percent opacity

## Material and lighting
- Matte clay, soft and slightly rough, gentle subsurface feel, no mirror reflections
- Key light from the top left, warm soft fill from the right
- No ground shadow baked into the file (we add the shadow in code)

## Camera
- Three quarter front view, camera slightly below eye level
- Identical framing for every pose
- Feet sit on the same baseline in every file so poses swap without jumping

## Poses (6 files)
1. idle: standing, calm smile
2. happy: wings lifted, bigger smile
3. eating: beak open, small seed near the beak
4. speaking: beak open, slight lean forward
5. sleeping: eyes closed as two soft curved lines, slight slump
6. excited: small hop, wings out

## Output
- PNG with transparent background, 1024 by 1024 pixels
- Character centered with about 10 percent padding on all sides
- Also export a WebP of each
- File names, all lowercase: idle.png, happy.png, eating.png, speaking.png, sleeping.png, excited.png
- Deliver into the folder public/pip/

## Optional second batch: 6 clay icons for the dashboards
Open book (stories), ear or sound wave (phonics), letter blocks (spelling), bar chart (progress), star (reward), trophy (streak).
Same palette, same lighting, same output spec, 512 by 512 pixels, saved into public/icons/.

## Do not
- No text, no logo, no background, no outlines, no glossy plastic look
